Nanny rescure is a site where working parents can log in and find the perfect nannies to help them take care of thier children. nannies subscribe to a site and pay a start up fee which takes care of background and criminal checks. The potential nannies must accept that nannyrescue is allowed to do background checks on them. Each Nannie maintains a public profile with pictures background information, nationality and some general personal details. When ther profile is set, Clients can now look into the database to find the perfect nanny for thir kids based on preferrence like nationality, race, sex or whatever floats thier boat.

Nannies without CRB checks can also register on the site but might not have the credibility to work. Clients will be warned about this and choosing such nannies will be at there own risk.

Nannies can have different ranks, Newbie Nanny, Intermidiate nanny, Expert Nanny and Super nanny.

Nannies can be voted up or down depending on the quality of the services they render to thier clients.
